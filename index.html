02 · Water on land

Inundation

For PV the dominant threat isn't surge — it's rainfall on the Sierra → flash flooding & river surge, compounded when the sea is high and the mouths can't drain. Three rivers into the bay: Cuale, Pitillal, Ameca; 24 flood-vulnerable points flagged in the 2026 Bahía de Banderas atlas.

Mechanism. When the Cuale runs high and the sea is high (king tide + long-period swell damming the mouth), the river can't discharge and backs up into the Cuale corridor / Zona Romántica / lower Olas Altas. Add a 6+ pop night and that's the flood. Never cross moving water — 12 in (30 cm) floats a car.

Why the zones sit where they sit. The sierra sheds; the flats receive. The Ameca floodplain (Ixtapa–Las Juntas–Pitillal strip) collects the whole valley corridor. The Cuale and Pitillal drop ~1,000 m in under 15 km, so an "over the bay" night is an arroyo night for Centro and Romántica — the pin sits between their two outlets. Bucerías and Sayulita flood at their crossings; the south-shore coves take short, violent debris-fan pulses; the headlands just drain.
