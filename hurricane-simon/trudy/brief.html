1. Where EP93 is and what the NHC says

NHC best-track fixes on Monday: 7.9N 93.1W at 6 am, 8.1N 93.9W at noon, 8.3N 95.3W at 6 pm, with 25-kt winds and 1008 mb at the last fix, about 1,700 km southeast of Puerto Vallarta. The 156-km move between noon and 6 pm is faster than the motion the outlook describes (about 16 km/h, or 100 km in six hours), which points to the broad center being re-fixed farther west rather than a real jump. The 5 pm PDT outlook: "Showers and thunderstorms are beginning to show signs of organization in association with a broad area of low pressure located several hundred miles south-southwest of the coast of Guatemala. This system is gradually becoming better defined, and environmental conditions are forecast to be conducive for further development. A tropical depression or tropical storm is expected to form well south of southern Mexico later tonight or tomorrow while the system moves generally west-northwestward at around 10 mph." Formation chance through 48 hours and 7 days: near 100 percent. The next name on the 2026 list is Trudy.

  • What an early version is. The NHC cannot wait for a run to finish, so at each cycle it takes the previous run and moves it so that its 6-hour position matches the new center, with the same shift at every hour. The 00Z DeepMind, track consensus and GEFS-mean lines here are the NHC's own; the Canadian mean is a 12-hour-old run moved the same way. No early AIFS exists in the NHC files, so we built one by the same rule from the 18Z AIFS run; the shift was 0.5° south and 1.0° west.
  • Two caveats about the five. The track consensus TVCN is computed at the NHC but is a plain average of the GFS, ECMWF, UKMET, HWRF, HMON, HAFS and COAMPS tracks, so it is kept; Figure 4 shows what changes if the corrected consensus is let back in. The AIFS, like every gridded AI model, under-forecasts intensity by construction, and the TVCN has no intensity at all; both carry the NHC intensity consensus IVCN, which is how the NHC uses TVCN.
  • What Simon's scores can and cannot say. They were earned at 12–36 h on a slow, stalling storm near the coast. Nothing guarantees they transfer to a six-day forecast of a storm that has not formed. Five lines cannot resolve a probability finer than 20%; the ensembles and the climatology are the context that keeps the five honest.
  • Center winds are not Vallarta's winds. Simon reached category 4 and passed 120 km away; the airport recorded 7 km/h and under 10 mm. The building numbers say "if the center followed that line"; once they exist, the NHC wind-speed probabilities are the number to use.

3. The lines on the map

The five still split the way all the guidance splits. The GEFS mean and the Canadian mean keep EP93 a weak system heading west; DeepMind, the AIFS and the track consensus make it a hurricane that turns north toward Cabo Corrientes from Friday, then pass it west or southwest of the bay on Sunday. Deeper storms are steered by deeper flow, so intensity and track are one question here. The ECMWF ensemble (12Z) puts 53% of 51 members within 300 km and 22% within 100 km; the AIFS ensemble (18Z) 78% and 31%; the GEFS (18Z) 6% and 0%. None of the three has been moved to the 6 pm center.

4. Climatology, for context

Of the 33 storms since 1949 that passed within 500 km of EP93's 6 pm position between 12 September and 11 November, 11 came within 300 km of Puerto Vallarta, 6 within 200 km and 3 within 100 km. 22 became hurricanes and 14 major hurricanes. The closest passes came 2.8–6.5 days after the analog point (one outlier at 16 days), which from Monday evening means Thursday to Monday. Climatology alone puts a 300-km pass at 33% and a 100-km pass at 9%.

7. When we will know

  • Tonight or Tuesday: NHC advisory 1 on Tropical Depression or Tropical Storm Trudy, with the first official five-day line and wind-speed probabilities. If Puerto Vallarta appears in the 34-kt probability table at all (the threshold is 3%), the close cases are alive in the official forecast.
  • Tuesday morning and evening: the 00Z and 12Z model runs, the first that start from the re-fixed center rather than being moved to it. If the five come back east toward the bay, the 6 pm re-fix was the outlier; if they stay west, the most-likely case firms up.
  • Thursday noon, about 72 hours out: the decision point. At three days the NHC's average track error is about 150 km. If the official line then runs within 150 km of the bay, prepare for the worst case; if it runs 250 km or more to the southwest, plan for swell and rain. Simon's lesson is that even inside 24 hours the line can shift 100 km; read the probabilities, not the line.
  • Friday: the SMN and the NHC would issue a Hurricane Watch for the Jalisco coast about 48 hours before tropical-storm winds are expected, if the close cases are still on the table.

8. Simon, meanwhile

NHC advisory 22A (Monday 6 pm Vallarta time): Simon is a category-2 hurricane with 85-kt winds about 125 km west of Cabo Corrientes, drifting west-northwest at 6 km/h. It is forecast to move slowly away from the bay, then turn northeast and reach the Sinaloa coast as a weakening hurricane around Thursday night or Friday. A Hurricane Warning is in effect for the Islas Marías and a Hurricane Watch from Cabo Corrientes to Bahía Tempehuaya. Puerto Vallarta's wind probabilities are below the 3% display threshold; Mazatlán's 34-kt cumulative probability is 54%. Simon will be inland over Sinaloa before Trudy's window opens; the swell it leaves on the Jalisco coast (4–5 m per SMN aviso 40) should ease by midweek, before Trudy's own swell arrives.

9. Method, sources and caveats

  • The five: Google DeepMind cyclone model (ATCF aids GDMN 18Z, GDMI 00Z), ECMWF AIFS single run (ECMWF open-data tropical-cyclone track files, 18Z, disturbance 71E, decoded with ecCodes; the 00Z early version built by the NHC rule), track consensus TVCN, Canadian ensemble mean (CEMI 18Z, CEM2 00Z), GEFS ensemble mean (AEMN 18Z, AEMI 00Z).
  • Intensity: each model's own, except the TVCN (none) and the AIFS (model 10-m wind, low by construction), which carry the NHC intensity consensus IVCN of their cycle, interpolated to each hour.
  • Closest approach, timing, landfall, building winds, rain: every track interpolated hourly; distance and timing to 20.61N 105.23W; landfall is the first hour over land in the Natural Earth 50 m polygons; wind from the parametric field of the Simon map page with the model's own 34/50/64-kt radii where the deck carries them and climatological radii otherwise, times the Simon exposure factors, with no decay over land; rain from an R-CLIPER-type profile (after Tuleya et al. 2007) integrated hourly, with no terrain enhancement.
  • Weights: inverse square of each model's 12–36 h mean position error over Simon's last 48 h (final report, Figure 2): DeepMind 33, AIFS 50, TVCN 51, Canadian ensemble mean 54, GEFS mean 57 km.
  • Context: GEFS members and all other aids from the NHC ATCF a-deck (aep932026.dat, as of 01Z on 13 October); best track bep932026.dat; ECMWF IFS ensemble (12Z, 51 members) and AIFS ensemble (18Z) from the ECMWF open-data track files (the 18Z IFS files stop at 144 h, short of the window); HURDAT2 NE Pacific 1949–2025 for the analogs, centred on the 6 pm position.
  • Not included: the real 00Z model runs (not yet published); any NHC advisory, because none exists. This document should be rerun when advisory 1 and the Tuesday runs are out.
